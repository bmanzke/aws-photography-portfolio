About

I’m Brett Manzke, a photographer based in Plainfield, Illinois. I chase two kinds of light: the small — macro views of flowers and plants most people walk right past — and the vast — eclipses, the Moon, and deep-sky targets from my own backyard. Stem and Stars is where both halves live.
